Document and Organization Management
Document Management — How Organized Are You Now?
Most caregivers find that knowing the existence or location of documents and various online accounts can be a time-consuming task. Pleaides provides the ability to track gaps and assign roles for management. Documents and account access information can be stored securely in your dashboard, or stored offline with the ability to easily view contact information.

Logistics — Scheduling, Calendar, Care Coordination
The scheduling & logistics calendar lets important daily scheduling be viewed and shared with others. For each task, specify what’s of value to view and share, and whether you’d like help managing and accepting tasks. Tasks can be organized by location, so a caregiver in another state won’t be asked to drive.
